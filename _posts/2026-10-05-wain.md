I've been busy setting up some infrustructure now that I feel like my self-hosting skills are up-to-par. I'm at a point where I can finally implement some services I've been wanting to offer for some time. The first will be an archive of the images on [catland.distin.org](https://catland.distin.org/). This was done via scraping the Internet Archive mirror of the wiki (at the recommendation of the site admins). This is a wiki cataloging high resolution scans of the collected works of [Louis Wain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Wain), an English artist who loved drawing cats. Every cat-themed profile picture I have is one of his works, and I thought it might be worthwile to give these scans some exposure so someone else could be similarly insipired. You can find the archive [here](https://homelab.farazkaleemmalik.cyou/nextcloud/index.php/s/SkM4QwrQZabetba), though you should really use catland for anything other than bulk downloading since mine is a stripped copy of a stripped copy of the wiki.

I compiled this archive using [StrawberryMaster's fork of wayback-machine-downloader](https://github.com/StrawberryMaster/wayback-machine-downloader), with a small modification due to the massive filenames of the scans (see [here](https://homelab.farazkaleemmalik.cyou/forgejo/faraz/wayback-machine-downloader)). The command (using the new option implemented in my fork) is
```bash
wayback_machine_downloader https://catland.distin.org/_images -p 1000 --limit-filenames
```
